Tilt & Learn

The head-tilt grammar game for two teams.

  1. 1. Say hi to the cameraClick Start and press Allow when the browser asks to use your camera.
  2. 2. Read the questionTwo answers appear: A on the left and B on the right.
  3. 3. Tilt and holdTilt your head to the left for A or to the right for B. Hold it until the bar is full!

Teams take turns. Only the team that answers correctly wins points. Works best in Chrome or Edge with an internet connection.
